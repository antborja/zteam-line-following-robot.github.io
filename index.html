Looking Forward

During competition week, we implemented chassis design changes that would help minimize robot wobble and improve steering adjustments. Throughout the different competitions, we noticed we had issues with power delivery, weight reduction, and on-the-fly PID adjustment. To improve our power delivery, we plan to adjust heavier components to be closer to the axis of rotationm, thus reducing the amount of torque needed to turn the robot. Additionally, strategically holes and making the light shield fit tight around the photoresistors will help reduce the weight. During the loop track, the rubber bands around the outer wheel would come loose due to the centripetal force. This can be fixed through perhaps getting a single, larger rubber band that has more surface area to grab onto the wheel and stay on.
